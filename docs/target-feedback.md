# Direct board target feedback

Choose a build action to see its legal locations in pale yellow. Move the pointer or keyboard focus over a target to preview it in orange. During construction, a preview of the piece shows how it will sit on the road or intersection. Nearby scenery recedes so the placement is readable at the coast.

With a mouse, click the legal location to build. In the SVG board view, keyboard players can focus a legal location and press Enter or Space to use it. On a touchscreen, tap a legal construction location and lift your finger to inspect the piece. Tap a different legal location to move the preview, then use the **Confirm** button beneath the board to place it. **Cancel selection** exits placement. Moving the robber still uses a direct board tap. Moving the camera or scrolling must never place a piece.

The preview is local to the player choosing the move. A confirmed move follows the usual game validation. Leaving the mode or losing the right to act clears the preview. Spectators see the completed move.

Browser QA exercises the board’s actual hit areas, including mouse, touch and SVG keyboard input, cancellation, camera preservation and multiplayer reconnect.
