# Harbor Co-op v3: cities, trade stations and two-resource sevens

Product Owner approval: 2026-11-21, building on the approved 2026-11-16 campaign. Task classification: complex, directly approved gameplay implementation. Supported: two humans online, local hotseat/solo control of both seats, or one human plus a local teammate Bot. Classic is unchanged. New games use harbor version 3. Existing version-1 and version-2 saves retain their recorded campaign rules ([v1](harbor-coop-v1.md), [v2](harbor-coop-v2.md)) until restarted. The 2026-11-27 unlimited-bank amendment applies to every version for subsequent actions in the updated client.

## Explicit user rules

- Development cards are removed: there is no deck, purchase, card play or development-card UI in new Co-op games.
- A trade station must be built at a vertex where roads from both players meet. It costs one wool, one grain and one ore, the Classic development-card purchase cost. Each station allows each player to give their partner one resource card per own turn.
- Paid roads require the acting player's own road or building. After setup, building a settlement also requires the acting player's own road directly attached to its vertex. A teammate's road or building cannot support construction; a trade station is not an ownership shortcut.
- Keep two snake-order placement opportunities per player. The first seat (seat 0) places one settlement and two roads each time. The second seat (seat 1) places one city each time, without free roads. Therefore the initial totals are two settlements/four roads versus two cities/no roads, independent of the randomly selected first mover. In local human-plus-Bot play, the human always occupies the city seat and the Bot always occupies the settlement-and-roads seat. Online seating and local hotseat retain their existing assignment.
- Rolling seven gives each partner two different random resource types, one card of each, from the unlimited bank. No discard, robber movement or theft follows the roll.
- Show the first delivery order as soon as the game starts, during placement. It requires two different resource types and allows seven complete team rounds.
- Thereafter add one new order every three team rounds. Requirements increase and deadlines shorten. Deliver ten of the twelve total orders to win together.

## Reversible implementation choices / first-play tuning

A round means one normal turn per partner. Setup does not consume deadline time. Order arrivals are rounds 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28, 31 and 34. The first order has one card of each of its two types. Total cards then increase by one per order: 2 through 13. Resource diversity rises from two to three to four to five types every three orders. Deadlines, including the arrival round, are 7, 7, 6, 6, 5, 5, 4, 4, 3, 3, 3, 3 rounds. An order due in round 7 remains deliverable through the second player's turn of round 7 and expires only when round 8 starts.

These numerical choices are tunable initial values, not a balance guarantee. New orders follow the clock, never immediate delivery replacements. Orders may overlap; a new arrival does not delete an older active order. Twelve is a total contract count, not a twelve-game-round time limit. Ten fulfilled orders immediately win; a third missed order immediately loses because ten successes are then impossible. Partial cargo returns to the bank on completion or expiration.

## Station behavior

At least one incident built road belongs to each partner at the exact station vertex. A remote connection elsewhere does not qualify. Either player can pay the full construction cost on their action turn. One station per vertex. Stations are separate communal infrastructure: no victory points, resource production, or settlement-piece consumption. They may coexist with a settlement/city at a junction and do not block normal building placement. No first-station discount is granted.

Build by clicking Station in Actions, then a highlighted vertex on the existing 3D or 2D board. Each built station has one resource-send button per available resource in the order panel. One send means exactly one resource card; the sender must own it. The per-station allowance is independent for both players, may be used immediately after construction, resets on that player's next turn, and cannot be banked. A second station gives one additional send per player turn.

Unlimited player offers/acceptance and gifts are disabled for v2 and v3: resources between partners must use the station command and allowance. Bank/port trades are unchanged. Station construction is a safely undoable deterministic action; a transfer clears undo eligibility. Duplicate/stale network commands are rejected by existing authority checks. Station allowance is saved and restored, so refresh/reconnect does not grant another send.

## Existing mechanics retained

Each player's hand, pieces and construction decisions remain separate. Harbor v2 and v3 cargo must travel from the loading player's own settlement/city to the destination port entirely on that player's own built roads. Even one partner-owned road invalidates that route. A station allows bounded card transfers only and never lends shipping access. A player's own building directly on the port still connects without a road; a partner's building never acts as a shipping origin. Route hints and the Bot treat partner-owned edges as impassable, not missing roads to be built over. Personal logistics action points remain out of scope. Orders still accept partial contributions from either connected partner's own hand. Road construction requires an owned endpoint building or an adjacent owned road at an unoccupied vertex. A teammate building is not a usable endpoint; the other endpoint may still provide a valid owned connection. Road networks may meet to form a station, but that station never lends ownership or creates construction support. Settlement construction requires an incident road owned by the builder; an incident teammate road alone never qualifies. The distance rule, resources and initial free placements remain unchanged. Archived Harbor v1 campaigns retain their previous shared-road settlement support. First-play starting bonus cards are retained. Second starting building grants adjacent resource cards: one per hex for a settlement, two for a city, from the unlimited bank.

In Harbor v3, a dice total of seven grants each partner two different random resource types, one card of each, then immediately enters the action phase. Nobody discards, including hands above seven; the robber stays exactly where it was and no theft occurs. Each draw selects uniformly among resource types not already awarded to that player on this roll. Partners may receive matching pairs, but neither player receives two cards of the same type. The unlimited bank supplies both cards to each partner, with draws starting with the roller. The signed bank ledger records every transfer and may be negative; it does not cap supply. This roll-only bonus does not consume trade-station quota. Team orders decide the outcome. Manual 3D view, orange hover, direct board picking, local and online restart/recovery and spectator privacy remain intact.

## Engineering and acceptance

Rules and quota validation are deterministic, host-authoritative and separate from presentation and transport. Station geometry is public-only; no private hand or network credential is passed to the renderer. Bots use their own hand plus public order/road data, can handle the asymmetric setup, build a useful station and make legal capped transfers. No paid service, remote AI, infrastructure redesign or main-branch merge is authorized.

Verification covers owner-only paid/free road support, shared-junction stations without road ownership leakage, legal-target/authority/Bot agreement, both starting seats, setup legality/supplies/conservation, station junction geometry and cost, co-located buildings, independent quotas across stations/players/turns, trade bypass rejection, safe Undo and non-undoable sends, authoritative duplicate/stale messages, checkpoint recovery, order arrivals/deadlines/overlap, escalation, finite twelve-order generation and ten-of-twelve outcomes. Browser tests exercise actual board construction and send buttons, mobile layout, 3D/2D fallback, Bot setup, online guests/spectators, reconnect and same-room restart. Test fixtures remain local-only and are excluded from production bundles.

## Version compatibility

Version 1 preserves its archived campaign and card rules. Version 2 retains development cards and their cooperative adaptations, the settlement-resource station cost (one lumber, brick, wool and grain), and one random resource per player on seven. Version 3 removes the development deck and rejects card purchase, play and free-road commands; a recovered v3 state cannot contain development cards or a pending card phase. Recovery never awards retroactive resources. Restart creates version 3, including a same-room online restart and local Bot restart.

Local Bot seat assignment is established when the local room starts and retained for restart. Human identity and private-hand view remain attached to the human, independent of the random first mover. The opening hint names the actual players assigned to each role.

## Historical 2026-11-16 shipping and seven amendment

User-approved changes: no borrowed roads for Load; seven grants one random resource to each player; no discards and no robber movement from that roll. These apply to Harbor v2, including local hotseat, the local teammate Bot and host-authoritative online play. Classic and archived v1 rules are unchanged. Existing v2 checkpoints without reward metadata remain valid; already-started old seven subphases are preserved on recovery and do not receive retroactive rewards. Restart for an unambiguous clean application of all current rules.

Rewards are generated once in the validated roll reducer using the host's entropy online and the existing persisted random stream locally. The public result contains only the newly awarded cards, not remaining private hands. UI and spectators use this result for resource-icon announcements rather than re-rolling. Duplicate/stale rolls, Undo of a roll, rendering and reconnect cannot repeat rewards. Metadata is cleared at end of turn; no game entropy is consumed to calculate shipping routes.

Implementation classification: complex (rule reducer, public result metadata, recovery validation, UI and Bot integration). No new service, permission, deployment platform or main-branch merge. Tests add owned-route, mixed-route, station, detour, blocked-route, seven-only resource grant, high hands, unlimited bank supply, identical rewards, Knight/Classic/v1 regression, public/private projection, duplicate/stale/reconnect and real browser-button checks.

## 2026-11-17 settlement ownership clarification

Product Owner approval: in the two-player cooperative mode, a player's settlement cannot use the teammate's road as its construction connection. New Harbor v2 games apply this to paid settlement builds for either player. The same ownership check drives legal target highlights, local Bot decisions and host-authoritative command validation. Trade stations do not grant road ownership. Setup placements, Classic and archived Harbor v1 campaigns retain their established rules. Added tests cover both players, rejected forged multiplayer commands, owned-road success, and version compatibility.
