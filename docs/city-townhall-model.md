# City model refinement

Approved 3D-art-only change: replace the asymmetric tall city block with an upgraded settlement silhouette: centered gabled main hall, two low roofed wings, a shallow foundation, arched entrance and restrained warm windows. The six player body colors are retained exactly. Roofs and foundation use related darker tints; trim is lighter.

City footprint is about 1.41 times settlement width and about 1.19 times its height. The model remains centered on the original vertex and below the existing city-target marker. Settlement geometry, port boats, terrain gradients, rules, supplies, upgrade costs, Undo, camera, transport and private data are unchanged. All reusable geometries/materials are owned and disposed by ArtAssets.
