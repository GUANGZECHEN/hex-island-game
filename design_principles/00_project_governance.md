# Project governance

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Product Owner:** Defines requirements and approves major decisions, significant changes, and product acceptance.
- **Architect (ChatGPT):** Defines architecture and technical decisions, creates technical task specifications and acceptance criteria, and conducts architecture reviews.
- **Engineer (Codex):** Implements approved tasks, tests, documents, and reports results in accordance with project instructions and approved principles.

Codex does not act as Product Owner or independently approve major design changes. Escalate conflicts and consequential ambiguities to the appropriate decision-maker before implementation.
